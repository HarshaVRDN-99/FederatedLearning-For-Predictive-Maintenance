# FederatedLearning-For-Predictive-Maintenance
Summary
We will build a reusable federated-learning (FL) pipeline that predicts Remaining Useful Life (RUL) on NASA C-MAPSS FD004. We then use it to measure how client heterogeneity affects FedAvg, and whether FedProx limits the damage.
Centralized and local-only training are baselines, not the contribution. The independent variable is the type and degree of non-IIDness across simulated industrial sites. Our contribution, in one sentence:
A controlled investigation of how different forms and degrees of non-IIDness affect federated RUL prediction, and whether FedProx mitigates the resulting degradation relative to FedAvg.
